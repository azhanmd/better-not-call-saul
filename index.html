The latest scams, and how to spot them

Better Not Call Saul raises awareness of the most recent, common and emerging scams. Each drill replays a real case, step by step, so you can practise spotting it before it reaches you.

  1. Emerging22 October 2026

    The call about your phone number

    Why it's emerging: The FTC calls it the latest twist on fake-FTC calls.

    A caller says your phone number is linked to scams abroad and threatens to cut it off.

    Phone call and text message · Government and police impersonation

    Targets:Anyone

    Source: Federal Trade Commission

  2. Recent21 October 2026

    The text about your 2027 increase

    A text says your Social Security increase for 2027 won't be paid until you activate it.

    Text message and phone call · Government and police impersonation

    Targets:Benefit recipients

    Source: Social Security's Office of the Inspector General

  3. Recent19 October 2026

    The call from the police

    A detective calls to say they've arrested a suspect who had your details.

    Phone call · Government and police impersonation

    Targets:Anyone

    Source: West Somerset Free Press

  4. Emerging10 September 2026

    The urgent payment from the CEO

    Why it's emerging: An old trick that AI is making more convincing.

    You work in accounts payable, and your company's email addresses end in @yourcompany.example. An email from the CEO asks you to pay an invoice today.

    Email · Business email compromise

    Targets:Businesses

    Source: Microsoft Threat Intelligence

  5. Emerging1 September 2026

    The delivery text from a van

    Why it's emerging: The first SMS blaster seized in the UK.

    A missed-delivery text arrives while you're out shopping, sent from a van parked nearby.

    Text message · Phishing

    Targets:Anyone

    Source: City of London Police
