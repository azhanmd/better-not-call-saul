Privacy

There are no accounts and no ads, and what you do in a drill never leaves your browser.

What you do in a drill

Your highlights, choices and answers stay in your browser while you play. They aren't saved or sent anywhere, and they're gone when you reload or leave the page.

The share button passes the drill's link and a line about your score to your device's share menu, or copies them for you to paste. Nothing is sent to us.

Light and dark mode

The site starts in dark mode. If you switch to light mode, your browser keeps a note of that choice in its local storage on your device, so other pages open in light mode too. It isn't a cookie, it's never sent anywhere, and switching back to dark mode deletes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs and stores the IP address of everyone who visits a GitHub Pages site, for security, whether or not they're signed in to GitHub. Read GitHub's note on data collection.

Visitor numbers

We don't use analytics, cookies or any other tracking.

Links to other sites

Drills link to the news reports and official advice they're based on. Those links open in a new tab, and your browser doesn't tell those sites which drill you came from. They have their own privacy policies.
