Online shopping scams

Fake shops and sellers take your money for things that never arrive. Links in scam texts, emails and social media posts lead to websites copied from real shops, often with offers that seem too good to be true.

How common it is

  • US56,478 complaints to the FBI in 2025 about goods or services that were paid for but never arrived, or weren't paid for.
  • UKPurchase scams hit record levels in 2025, and they often start on online marketplaces.
  • US$503.4 million was reported lost in the US in 2025.

Warning signs

  • !Offers that seem too good to be true, often through links in texts, emails or social media posts.
  • !A website address that's almost, but not quite, the real shop's.
  • !Being asked to pay by bank transfer. Paying by credit card gives you more protection.

Who it targets

  • Online shoppers

Practise spotting it

There's no drill for this scam yet.

What to do

  • UKLost money or shared your details? Report it to Report Fraud online or on 0300 123 2040. In Scotland, call Police Scotland on 101. Source: Stop! Think Fraud (UK Government)
  • USPaid a fake request? Contact your bank straight away to ask for a recall, and report it to the FBI at ic3.gov. Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center
