No. 1 of the 6 most common scams

Phishing

Scam emails, texts and calls pretend to come from a company or organisation you trust, to get your personal or financial details or your login details.

How common it is

  • US191,561 complaints to the FBI in 2025.
  • USPeople aged 60 and over made 48,064 of these complaints, about twice as many as in 2024.
  • US$215.8 million was reported lost in 2025, about three times as much as in 2024.

Warning signs

  • !It claims to be from someone official, such as your bank or a government department.
  • !It gives you a deadline, such as 24 hours, or threatens a fine.
  • !It asks for personal details by email, or a caller asks you to confirm your bank details. Your bank never will.

Who it targets

  • Anyone
  • Older people

What to do

  • UKForward scam emails to report@phishing.gov.uk. The NCSC checks the email and any websites it links to. Source: National Cyber Security Centre
  • UKForward scam texts to 7726. It's free, and your mobile provider can block the sender. Source: National Cyber Security Centre
  • USForward scam texts to 7726 (SPAM). Source: Federal Trade Commission
  • USReport scams to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Source: Federal Trade Commission
