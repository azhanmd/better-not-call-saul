Tech support scams

Scammers pose as technical or customer support, such as a computer help desk, your bank, an online shop or a utility company. They try to get you to call them, install software, or hand over control of your device or accounts.

How common it is

  • US47,794 complaints to the FBI in 2025, with $2.1 billion lost.
  • USPeople aged 60 and over made 21,333 of these complaints.
  • The FBI says the same criminals often run tech support and government impersonation scams.

Warning signs

  • A pop-up, text or email tells you to call a number for help.
  • You're rushed to act fast to protect your device.
  • Someone who contacted you asks you to install software or let them control your device.
  • A support number you found by searching may be a scammer's paid advert.

Who it targets

  • Older people

Practise spotting it

There's no drill for this scam yet.

What to do

  • USPaid a fake request? Contact your bank straight away to ask for a recall, and report it to the FBI at ic3.gov. Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center
  • UKLost money or shared your details? Report it to Report Fraud online or on 0300 123 2040. In Scotland, call Police Scotland on 101. Source: Stop! Think Fraud (UK Government)
